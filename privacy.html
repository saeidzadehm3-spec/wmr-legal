Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The "World Market Report Publisher" tool is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about the public or about viewers.

Data we handle

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the tool stores only the access token needed to upload the owner's own videos. The token is kept in a private secrets store, is used only for uploads the owner has approved, and can be revoked by the owner at any time in the TikTok settings.

What we do not do

We do not read, store or analyze other users' data, we do not use data for advertising, and we do not share data with third parties.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the World Market Report channel profile.
